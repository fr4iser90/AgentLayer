# Team onboarding — first week (Lernnotiz)

**Status:** self-authored learning note  
**Disclaimer:** learning aid — not an official company policy

## Week 1 checklist

1. Meet your team lead and get access to shared tools.
2. Read published notes in the knowledge companion before asking repeat questions.
3. Confirm which content categories apply to your role.

## Tip

Use **Knowledge Companion** chat to search published team notes. Draft and in-review content is not visible in production search.
